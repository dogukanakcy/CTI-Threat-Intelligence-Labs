## 📌 Key Concepts Learned
- **Data Decoding & Deobfuscation:** Decoding Base64, Hex, URL encoding, and Rot13 to reveal obfuscated threat actor payloads and C2 commands.
- **IoC Extraction & Defanging:** Extracting IP addresses, URLs, and domains from raw text while defanging them (e.g., `http[:]//example[.]com`) to prevent accidental execution.
- **Operations & Recipes:** Creating automated multi-step operations (recipes) for parsing raw intelligence data efficiently.
- **Hashing & File Analysis:** Generating MD5, SHA-1, and SHA-256 hashes for binary analysis and threat intelligence matching.
